How it works

A workflow you can inspect.

SCIOS separates discovery, evaluation, execution, and learning. That separation prevents a generated idea from being mistaken for an observed result.

  1. 01

    Define the objective

    The operator supplies an intent, constraints, and any known evidence. Unknowns remain visible.

  2. 02

    Evaluate the path

    Specialized services analyze the proposal and policy gates determine whether it can proceed, pause, or stop.

  3. 03

    Execute within bounds

    Approved integrations perform the requested action and return their actual provider response.

  4. 04

    Measure and reevaluate

    Observed outcomes are written back to durable memory for later review and better-grounded decisions.

Optional YouTube integration

Publish to an authorized channel—with a limited permission.

When an operator connects a Google account, SCIOS requests the youtube.upload OAuth scope only so its configured commercial workflow can upload video files to that operator’s YouTube channel.

  • SCIOS does not ask for a Google or YouTube password.
  • OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in the operator-controlled SCIOS environment.
  • SCIOS does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising profiles.
  • Access can be revoked from the Google Account permissions page at any time.

Trust by design

Clear limits are part of the product.

SCIOS reports configuration gaps, unavailable providers, and incomplete evidence instead of converting them into claims of success.

01

No invented outcomes

Forecasts and operator estimates are labeled separately from observed customer, revenue, or deployment data.

02

Operator-controlled integrations

External services work only when the operator configures the required credentials and grants the relevant authority.

03

Revocable access

Third-party authorization can be withdrawn, and local credential files can be removed by the operator.

04

Published policies

Privacy and terms are publicly available without signing in and apply to the SCIOS application and website.
